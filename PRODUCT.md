# Product

**SnippetVault** is a private, realtime, offline-first vault for the code a developer keeps rewriting.

- **Who:** a developer who saves snippets, commands and notes and needs them back in seconds.
- **Core jobs:** find a snippet by a keystroke, copy it, edit it, share a read-only link, and keep an
  encrypted vault for secrets.
- **Surfaces:** a desktop-first workspace with a responsive layout for phone browsers. The native
  Android app was removed and will be rebuilt from scratch later.
- **Tone:** quiet, precise, trustworthy. It is a tool for daily work, not a showcase.
- **Mode:** Operate. Scanability, consistency and speed matter more than expression. Brand lives in
  precise details.
